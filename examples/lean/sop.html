Measurement bench: sample to reviewed DistroFit analysis

Standard operating procedure (SOP), revision 1.

Keep sample identity, instrument checks and held measurements traceable before comparing distributions. This is a synthetic example to adapt and approve locally.

Start at Receive and identify samples. Follow the labelled routes, including holds and rechecks. The list order is a reference, not permission to skip a decision.

1. Receive and identify samples

Responsible role: Bench operator

Record the batch and sample IDs. Put incoming samples in the labelled incoming tray. Separate unknown or damaged samples for review.

Record: Incoming sample log

  • Next: Prepare the bench with the 6S checklist

2. Prepare the bench with the 6S checklist

Responsible role: Bench operator

Remove unnecessary items, return tools to labelled homes, clean measuring surfaces and confirm access to guards and emergency equipment. Record findings and resolve unsafe conditions before work.

Record: Dated 6S audit and action record

  • Next: Instrument and method ready?

3. Instrument and method ready?

Responsible role: Bench operator

Check instrument identity, calibration status, units and the current approved measurement method. Confirm the area is safe to operate.

Record: Instrument ID, calibration status and method revision

  • Yes: Measure and record original values
  • No: Hold the samples and resolve the issue

4. Hold the samples and resolve the issue

Responsible role: Area lead

Label and segregate held samples. Record the issue, responsible role and due date. Use a suitable instrument or obtain the required review before rechecking readiness.

Record: Hold log and corrective action evidence

  • Recheck after resolution: Instrument and method ready?

5. Measure and record original values

Responsible role: Bench operator

Follow the approved method. Record sample IDs, original readings and units without replacing the originals. Keep held and accepted samples in separate labelled locations.

Record: Original measurement log

  • Next: Measurement log complete and valid?

6. Measurement log complete and valid?

Responsible role: Reviewer

Check sample identity, units, transcription and method compliance. Investigate unusual readings; do not delete them only to improve the fitted curve.

Record: Review notes and any exclusions with reasons

  • Yes: Compare curves in DistroFit
  • No: Resolve missing or questionable readings

7. Resolve missing or questionable readings

Responsible role: Bench operator and reviewer

Retain the original log and document the reason for corrections or repeat measurements. Keep affected samples on hold until the reviewer accepts the record.

Record: Correction or repeat-measurement record

  • Review again: Measurement log complete and valid?

8. Compare curves in DistroFit

Responsible role: Analyst

Load accepted numeric values, confirm units, choose a held-out fraction and compare fitted curves. Use SPC for time-ordered process checks; record preprocessing and limits before interpreting results.

Record: Saved DistroFit comparison with input data and settings

  • Next: Archive the reviewed result and reset the bench

9. Archive the reviewed result and reset the bench

Responsible role: Reviewer and bench operator

Save the reviewed report with the batch record. Return tools and reference samples, clean the area and schedule follow-up on 6S actions. A fitted curve does not authorize product release.

Record: Reviewed analysis, bench reset and follow-up record

End of this route.
